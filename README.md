# 🎓 Student Skills & Services Platform

A full-stack web platform connecting university students to trade skills, offer services, and collaborate. Students can offer or book peer tutoring, language practice, graphic design, photography, CV writing support, and more—complete with real-time filtering, instant cost estimates, and validated booking workflows.

---

## 🌟 Key Features

* **Skill & Service Listings**: Explore student-offered services across categories like Academic Tutoring, Language Exchange, Media & Design, and Career Services.
* **Smart Filtering**: Filter available services by skill type, price range, and specific calendar availability/time slots.
* **Real-Time Cost Estimator**: Dynamically calculates service costs based on selected duration (hours), service rate, or service packages before booking.
* **Validated Booking System**: Checks tutor/service provider availability and validates schedules to prevent double-booking or scheduling conflicts.
* **Student Profiles**: Showcase peer ratings, reviews, past completed services, and verified student status.
