Student Skills & Services Platform

University life is full of challenges, and every student has both needs and talents.
Some students struggle with difficult courses, while others are looking for a partner to practice a new language.
At the same time, many students possess valuable skills such as graphic design, photography, or professional CV writing.
A student skills and services platform connects these two sides: students who need help, and students who can offer it.

The platform acts as a marketplace inside the campus community.
It allows students to publish the services they offer — such as peer tutoring in mathematics, conversation
practice in English or French, design work for posters and presentations, photography
for events, or support in building a strong CV — and allows other students to browse, compare, and book those services easily and safely.

Services Offered

The platform covers a wide range of student-to-student services. Peer tutoring helps students who need extra explanation in subjects like programming, statistics, or physics, with sessions delivered by classmates who already mastered the material. Language practice connects learners with fluent speakers for conversation sessions that improve fluency and confidence in a relaxed environment.

Beyond academic help, the platform also supports creative and professional services. Design services include posters, social media graphics, and presentation templates. Photography services cover event coverage, graduation photos, and portrait sessions. Finally, CV support offers students help in writing, formatting, and polishing their resumes before applying for internships or jobs. This variety makes the platform useful for every student, regardless of their field of study.
